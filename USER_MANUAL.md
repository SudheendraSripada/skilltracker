# 🚀 Skill Tracker: Official User Guide

Welcome to **Skill Tracker**! Your ultimate learning command center designed to help you plan, practice, and prove mastery across any topic. This guide will walk you through everything from the absolute basics to utilizing our advanced interactive document AI.

---

## 🌟 1. Getting Started

### Creating Your Account
To start tracking your progress, you'll need an account.
1. Navigate to the landing page.
2. Click the 🖱️ **Open app** or 🖱️ **Launch Skill Tracker** button.
3. Sign up using your email and a secure password. *(Note: Your session securely tracks your daily Check-ins and Streaks!)*

---

## 📚 2. Building Your Learning Track

Skill Tracker isn't just a list—it's a structured path. 

### Adding a New Topic
1. Once inside the app dashboard, locate the input bar.
2. Type in whatever you want to learn (e.g., *"Machine Learning Basics"*).
3. Click 🖱️ **Generate Path**. 
*The system will automatically organize your topic into structured milestones and break those down into manageable subtopics.*

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** Each subtopic contains curated online resources, such as YouTube videos and articles, so you don't have to hunt for study materials!

---

## 📄 3. Interactive Document Learning (New!)

Rather than just reading passive text, you can upload your own study materials and talk to them!

### Uploading a PDF
1. Navigate to your selected Topic workspace.
2. Look for the document area and click 🖱️ **+ Upload PDF**.
3. Select any standard PDF from your computer. 

*(Free plan users can upload up to 1 document per topic. To unlock unlimited uploads, check out our Premium tier!)*

### Studying with AI (Split-Pane View)
Once uploaded, click on your document to enter the **Interactive Studio**:
- **Left Side:** A clean, easy-to-read PDF viewer displaying your exact document.
- **Right Side:** Your AI Tutor Chat. 
  - Click the 🖱️ **Chat box** and ask a question like *"Explain page 3 clearly"* or *"Summarize the core arguments of this paper."*
  - The AI reads your document instantly and answers using *only* the context provided in the PDF!

### Generating Practice Quizzes
Did you finish reading? Prove your mastery!
1. Within the Interactive Studio, click 🖱️ **Practice Quiz**.
2. The AI will instantly generate a single-attempt test tailored entirely around the specific concepts of the document you just uploaded.

## 💎 4. Upgrading to Premium

Want to supercharge your learning?
1. Try uploading a second document or switching your AI model to **GPT-4o**.
2. You will see our sleek **Pro Upgrade Modal**. 
3. Click 🖱️ **Upgrade to Pro** to unlock:
